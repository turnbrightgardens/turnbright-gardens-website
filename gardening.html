Gardening FAQs

Gardening questions

Answers to common questions about our gardening services in Barnet, Enfield, North London and nearby areas.

How much does garden maintenance cost?

Garden maintenance has a £120 minimum charge, which covers the first 2 hours with a team of 2 gardeners. Additional time is £60 per hour.

How much does a garden clearance cost?

Garden clearance has a £150 minimum charge, which covers the first 2 hours with a team of 2 gardeners. Additional time is £75 per hour.

What is the difference between garden maintenance and garden clearance?

Maintenance is routine upkeep for regularly maintained gardens, such as mowing, edging, light pruning and light weeding. Clearance is heavier work, such as overgrown lawns, heavy hedge cutbacks, significant weed removal and neglected flower beds.

Is green waste removal included?

Green waste removal is charged separately unless it is included in your quote: £15 per 120-litre green sack, or £60 per ton or 1000-litre jumbo bag, with bags provided. If you prefer, we can leave it in an agreed place on site.

How much does pressure washing cost?

Pressure washing is £3.50 per m², with a minimum charge of £120 (covers up to 34 m²). We clean patios, decking and paths.

Do you look after commercial properties?

Yes. We provide gardening, grounds care and pressure washing for offices, shops, rental properties, landlords, estate agents and commercial sites. Regular maintenance and one-off commercial jobs are quoted individually.

Do you cover Barnet, Enfield and North London?

Yes. We cover Barnet, Enfield and the rest of North London, plus other parts of Greater London and nearby towns such as Watford, St Albans and Borehamwood. See our Areas page for the full list.

Are you insured, and are quotes free?

Yes. TurnBright is fully insured and quotes are free. For the fastest quote, send photos of your garden, your postcode and what you would like done.

FAQs

House clearance questions

How much does house clearance cost?

Prices start from £50 for a small pile of waste. Each job is priced on volume, access, floor level, parking and disposal. Send us photos for an exact quote before any work begins.

How do I get a quote?

Call 020 8064 1412, send photos on WhatsApp to 07877 056734, or use our contact form. Include photos of the items, the floor level, access details and your postcode. Quotes are free.

What can you clear?

Furniture, appliances, boxes, bags, household items, mixed waste and bulky waste from homes, garages and commercial premises, plus full house, flat and rental property clearances. Unsure about an item? Send us a photo.

Can you collect just one item?

Yes. We can remove single bulky items such as a sofa or an appliance, as well as larger mixed-waste loads and full property clearances. See rubbish removal for single items and smaller loads.

Which areas do you cover?

We cover the London boroughs of Barnet, Enfield, Haringey, Islington, Camden, Hackney, Brent, Westminster and Kensington and Chelsea, plus towns such as Watford, St Albans, Borehamwood, Harrow and Cheshunt. See our Areas page or send us your postcode.

Are you insured and licensed?

Yes. TurnBright is fully insured and a registered waste carrier with the Environment Agency, licence number CBDU429791. You can check it on the Environment Agency public register.

Do you work with landlords, estate agents and businesses?

Yes. We clear homes, rental properties and commercial sites for homeowners, landlords, estate agents and businesses.

Do you offer probate and bereavement clearances?

Yes. We clear a loved one’s home after they pass away, working at your pace. We can collect keys from families, executors or solicitors, and set aside documents, photos and valuables for the family.

How quickly can you come?

We can often attend the same day, and sometimes within 2 hours, depending on our availability and your location. For the quickest quote, send us photos of what needs clearing and your postcode.

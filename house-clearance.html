FAQs

House clearance questions

How much does house clearance cost?

Prices start from £50 for a small pile of waste. Every job is priced on the amount of waste, access, floor level, parking and disposal requirements. Send us photos and we will give you an exact quote before any work begins.

How do I get a quote?

Call 020 8064 1412, send photos on WhatsApp to 07877 056734, or use our contact form. Include photos of the items, the floor level, access details and your postcode. Quotes are free.

What can you clear?

We remove furniture, appliances, boxes, bags, general household items, mixed waste, general rubbish and bulky waste from homes, garages and commercial premises, as well as full house, flat and rental property clearances. If you are unsure about an item, send us a photo and ask.

Can you collect just one item?

Yes. We can remove single bulky items such as a sofa or an appliance, as well as larger mixed-waste loads and full property clearances.

Which areas do you cover?

We cover Barnet, Enfield and the rest of North London, as well as Harrow, Wembley, Haringey, Ealing, Ilford and other parts of Greater London, plus nearby towns such as Watford, St Albans, Borehamwood, Potters Bar and Hemel Hempstead. See our Areas page for the full list, or send us your postcode to check.

Are you insured and licensed?

Yes. TurnBright is fully insured and a registered waste carrier with the Environment Agency, licence number CBDU429791. You can check it on the Environment Agency public register.

Do you work with landlords, estate agents and businesses?

Yes. We clear homes, rental properties and commercial sites for homeowners, landlords, estate agents and businesses.

How quickly can you come?

We can often attend the same day, and sometimes within 2 hours, depending on our availability and your location. For the quickest quote, send us photos of what needs clearing and your postcode.

FAQs

Rubbish removal questions

How much does rubbish removal cost?

Small loads start from £50. Green waste is £15 per sack or £60 per ton or jumbo bag. Larger loads are by quote only, so send photos for a free price before any work begins.

Can you come the same day?

Often, yes. Same-day collection is often available, and sometimes within 2 hours, depending on our availability and your location.

What rubbish do you take?

Bulky items such as sofas, mattresses, fridges and furniture, bagged and mixed household waste, garden waste, and light builders’ waste. Unsure about an item? Send us a photo.

Do you collect single items?

Yes. We collect single bulky items like a sofa, mattress or fridge, as well as larger mixed loads.

Which areas do you cover?

Barnet, Enfield and the rest of North London, plus wider London and the Home Counties. Send your postcode to check availability.

Are you a licensed waste carrier?

Yes. TurnBright is registered with the Environment Agency as a waste carrier, licence number CBDU429791. You can check it on the public register.

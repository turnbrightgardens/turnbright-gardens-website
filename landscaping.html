Landscaping FAQs

Landscaping questions

Answers to common questions about fencing, turf, decking and gravel-grid parking in North and Central London, Hertfordshire and Essex.

How much does landscaping cost?

Every job is quoted individually and quotes are free. We price turf from measurements and photos, decking and fencing by size, access and materials, and gravel-grid parking by preparation, drainage and finish. Quotes are valid for 30 days unless stated otherwise.

How do I get a landscaping quote?

Send us measurements, photos and the finish you want by WhatsApp or email, or call 020 8064 1412. This lets us quote properly for the work.

Do you lay rolled turf and artificial turf?

Yes. We install rolled turf and artificial turf. We prepare the area, lay the turf neatly and leave the space ready to use.

Will artificial grass settle after it is installed?

Natural settlement of the sub-base can happen after installation. Minor settlement within the first few months is normal and does not necessarily mean the work is faulty.

Do you install fencing and decking?

Yes. We install new and replacement garden fencing for clearer boundaries and improved privacy, and decking areas for seating, pathways and outdoor use.

Can you create an extra parking space?

Yes. We have created extra parking by clearing and preparing the area, installing a supportive gravel grid and finishing it with decorative chippings. Each job is quoted on preparation, drainage and finish.

Do you cover North and Central London, Hertfordshire and Essex?

Yes. We cover the London boroughs of Barnet, Enfield, Haringey, Islington, Camden, Hackney, Brent, Westminster and Kensington and Chelsea, plus towns such as Watford, St Albans, Borehamwood, Harrow and Cheshunt. See our Areas page for the full list.

Are you insured, and do you work for landlords and businesses?

Yes. TurnBright is fully insured, and our landscaping work is suitable for homeowners, landlords, rental properties and commercial premises.

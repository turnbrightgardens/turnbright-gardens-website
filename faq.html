Common questions

House clearance FAQs

How much does house clearance cost?

House clearance is priced by volume, from £50 for a small pile of waste. Send us a few photos for an exact quote.

How much does house clearance cost in Barnet?

The same volume-based pricing applies in Barnet, from £50. WhatsApp photos to 07877 056734 for a quick quote.

Can you help clear a parent's house after death?

Yes. We handle probate clearances and can collect keys from family, executors or solicitors. We set aside documents, photos and valuables we find so nothing important is lost.

Do you clear council houses after a death?

Yes. Tell us the date the property needs to be empty and send photos, and we will quote and confirm availability.

How long does it take to clear a house after death?

It depends on the volume. We can often attend the same day, sometimes within 2 hours. Send photos and we will give you a quote and timing.

Can you quote from photos?

Yes. WhatsApp your photos to 07877 056734 and we will reply with a quote.

Do you take garden waste?

Yes. Garden waste is £15 per sack, and full garden clearance starts from £150.

Which areas do you cover?

Towns: Borehamwood, Broxbourne, Bushey, Cheshunt, Edgware, Epping, Harlow, Harrow, Loughton, Potters Bar, Radlett, St Albans, Stanmore, Waltham Abbey, Waltham Cross, Watford, Whitechapel and Woodford. Postcodes: E4, E17, EN4, EN5, N3, N16, N20, NW3, NW4, NW6, NW7 and W5.
